Overrun

The time after handover is still work.

Overrun is a ten-second log for doctors: when you were rostered, when you actually left, why, and who asked you to stay — then a claim pack you can hand to payroll, your JMO unit or your guardian of safe working.

A record, not advice — and no patient data

Overrun is an independent app. It is not connected to, endorsed by or acting for any hospital, health service, NHS body, rostering or payroll provider, medical union or government. It adds up the times you enter and compares them with limits you set. It is not legal, industrial or financial advice, and it does not decide whether any time is payable, how much, or under which agreement — your contract or enterprise agreement, your payroll team and your union do. Built-in starting values for working-hours limits are summaries of public documents on the effective date and may not match your contract; you can change every one.

Never enter patient names, record numbers, dates of birth or anything that identifies a patient. Reasons are general categories on purpose.

What it does

  • Asks how each rostered shift went, with Left on time, Leaving now and Still here right on the notification.
  • Counts extra time as minutes started before the rostered start plus minutes finished after the rostered end. Leaving early is shown, but never netted off another day. A callback counts in full.
  • Keeps a running total of extra time not yet claimed, by pay period, and — if you set one, as England's 28-day exception-reporting window — warns you a week before it closes.
  • Builds a PDF and a CSV for any pay period: rostered and actual times, reasons, who requested the work, missed meal breaks, and a signature line.
  • Shows how far your roster is from your real week, which shifts and reasons run over, and where your hours crossed the limits you set (longest shift, shortest rest, most hours in 7 days, weekly average).

What it does not do

  • It does not connect to StaffLink, ESR, any rostering system or payroll, and it does not submit claims or exception reports for you.
  • It does not work out overtime pay under an award or contract. If you enter an hourly rate it shows your own estimate, labelled as one.
  • It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising code.

Where the data lives

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. The other side of that, said plainly: there is no copy anywhere, so losing the phone loses the record. Keep your iPhone backup on, and export your claim packs as you go.
