Privacy

Effective September 19, 2026

The short version

Overrun collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising code, and no connection to any hospital, rostering or payroll system. What you type stays on your iPhone. We cannot see it, and neither can your employer unless you send them an export yourself.

1. What the app stores, and where

On your device only: each shift's rostered and actual times, the reasons you chose, whether a meal break was missed, who requested the work (as you describe them), a ward or unit, an optional note, the claim status you set, and your settings — including, if you enter them, your name, grade, hospital and an hourly rate for the claim pack. It is not sent anywhere.

Together that shows where and when you work and what you are owed, which is exactly the kind of record an employment dispute can turn on. That is one of the reasons there is no server.

2. What we collect

Nothing. No account, no email address, no device identifier, no usage statistics, no crash reporting service. The app makes no network requests of its own. The App Store privacy label for Overrun is Data Not Collected.

3. Patient information

The app asks you never to enter anything that identifies a patient, and its reasons are general categories. If you type patient details into a free-text field anyway, they stay on your device and in any export you make; you are responsible for them under your professional and legal duties.

4. Apple

Buying a subscription is a transaction between you and Apple. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active; it does not tell us who you are. Apple's handling of that purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy. If you contact us by email, we see what you choose to send.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the device by the app itself. Nothing goes through a push server. A reminder may mention a rostered finish time or an amount of unclaimed time; it never contains a ward, a name or a note, because lock screens are visible to other people. If you use a button on a reminder, the app records only that answer against that shift.

6. Exports

The PDF and spreadsheet are created on the device. Where they go afterwards is entirely your choice.

7. Children

Overrun is for working doctors. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.

8. Your rights

Privacy law gives you rights over personal information an organisation holds about you — including under the Australian Privacy Act, New Zealand's Privacy Act, the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR where they apply. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct or delete. Your own copy is on your device: every entry can be edited or deleted, and deleting the app removes the lot, immediately and permanently.

9. Data breaches

We hold no user data, so a breach of our systems could not expose yours. The data on your iPhone is protected by the device passcode and iOS. Keep a passcode on, and be careful where you send an export.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes and the current version always lives at https://inat97.github.io/overrun/privacy.html.
